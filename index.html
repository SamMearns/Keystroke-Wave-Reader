How it works
  1. Pick a part. Under “Record into”, choose Drums, Synths, Piano, Percussion or Effects.
  2. Write and record. Type or paste some text, then press Record. Every letter is a sound.
  3. It becomes a loop. When the text finishes, it is saved as that part’s loop.
  4. Fill the other parts. Repeat for each part, then choose a tempo and chords.
  5. Arrange and play. Click bars to bring parts in or out, then press Play to hear all 12 bars.
